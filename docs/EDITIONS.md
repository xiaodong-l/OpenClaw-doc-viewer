## FAQ / 常见问题

### English

**Q: Can I use Community Edition commercially?**

A: Yes! The MIT license allows commercial use. However, enterprise features require a commercial license.

**Q: What happens if I exceed Community Edition limits?**

A: There are no hard limits. Upgrade to Enterprise for advanced features and support.

**Q: Can I migrate from Enterprise to Community?**

A: Yes, but you'll lose access to enterprise-only features.

**问：我可以将社区版用于商业用途吗？**

**问：如果我超出社区版限制会怎样？**

**问：我可以从企业版迁移到社区版吗？**

答：可以，但您将无法使用企业版独占功能。
